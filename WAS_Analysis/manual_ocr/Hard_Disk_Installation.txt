INSTRUCTIONS FOR HARD DISK INSTALLATION

lf your computer has a hard disk you can install the Ancient Art of
War at Sea on it by performing the following steps:

:.

oe

Start up your computer using DOS 2.0 or higher.

Set your active drive and current working directory to the
location where you would like to install the Ancient Art of
War at Sea (for example C:\AAWS). If you need more
information on creating and using directories, please
consult your DOS manual.

Insert the Ancient Art of War at Sea disk in drive A and
close the drive door.

Type COPY A:*.* and press ENTER. This will copy all of
the files from the Ancient Art of War at Sea disk onto your
hard disk.

To run the program from the hard disk make sure the Ancient Art
of War at Sea floppy disk is in drive A and the C> prompt is
displayed on the screen (if it isn't, type C: and press ENTER),
then type WAS and press RETURN.

The copy protection scheme for the Ancient Art of War at Sea
requires that the original floppy disk be in drive A when the
program initially starts up. Once the title page is displayed you
may remove the floppy disk from drive A.
