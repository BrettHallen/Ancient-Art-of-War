# Tools for WAS: DOSBox

Runs the original WAS.EXE in DOSBox with the WAR harness (`../../../WAR_Analysis/tools/dosbox`: `dosrun2.sh`, `doskey.sh`, `dosshot.sh` and `screentext.py` work unchanged with `CONF=dosbox_was.conf`).

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `setup_was.sh` | copies the At Sea disk to `$DOS/was` (default `/root/dos`) and patches that copy of WAS.EXE at file offset 7AB3h (`75 07` -> `EB 07`) so it starts without the original floppy (the key-disk check, ovl1_a9a9); installs the confs; writes `$DOS/font8.txt` (copy it next to `screentext.py`; it is the game's font, so it is not kept in the repo) |
| `dosbox_was.conf` | machine=cga, 1500 cycles, mounts `$DOS/was` as A: and runs WAS.EXE |
| `dosbox_was_xt.conf` | the same at 300 cycles (about an IBM PC/XT): use it for timings; keys must then be held about 0.6 s (`HOLD=0.6`) |
| `dosbox_was_herc.conf` | `machine=hercules`, runs `WAS.EXE HERC` (screenshots are 720x348) |
| `setup_was_b.sh` | a second copy with a data disk as B: (`CONF=dosbox_was_b.conf`): WAS.EXE patched at 7AC2h and A2A3h so it uses B: without two floppy drives, SCNF's drive set to B: (step 6: saving and loading) |
| `setup_wast.sh` | the Tandy version: copies the disk to `$DOS/wast`, unpacks WAST.EXE to WASTU.EXE (`../disassembly/unexepack.py`) and patches its key-disk check; installs `dosboxx_wastu.conf` (DOSBox-X, `machine=tandy`, 1500 cycles). DOSBox 0.74 runs it too, but does not show the battle's palette registers |
| `xrun.sh`, `xshot.sh NAME`, `xkey.sh KEY...`, `xgoto.py X CY` | as `dosrun2.sh`, `dosshot.sh`, `doskey.sh` and `wasgoto.py`, for DOSBox-X (which leaves stale windows; `xshot.sh` takes the picture from the screen, under DOSBox-X's menu bar) |
| `wast_move.py WAST.DAT N S X Y ...` | moves squadrons in campaign SN inside a copy of WAST.DAT (WAST.EXE reads its campaigns from there, not the loose S files) |
| `wasplay.sh` | starts WAS.EXE and presses Enter five times (go to war, the first title, its story, the rules, the opponent): the map of The Bane of Oldain |
| `findcur.py SHOT` | where the marker is on a screenshot or video frame |
| `wasgoto.py X CY` | moves the marker to byte X, line pair CY by tapping the keypad and reading the screen |
| `make_font8.py` | the game's font for `screentext.py` (the same as WAR's) |

At 1500 cycles a key held for WAR's default 0.12 s is read more than once (T moves the time on two steps): use `HOLD=0.02` for single presses.

## Checked this way (12 Nov 2026)
- The scrolls are shown in CGA palette 0, everything else in palette 1.
- The first campaign's start, the command line over a squadron, Info, Fleet and the Surrender question match the remake pixel for pixel (`WAS_Remake/test/test_play.c` makes the same screens).
- The main loop at 300 cycles: with a key held the marker moves 27 bytes a second (video at 30 fps), so the remake runs campaign play at 27 iterations a second.
- Step 3 (the rules), with `wasplay.sh`, `wasgoto.py` and the WAR harness's `doskey.sh` / `dosshot.sh` / `screentext.py`: tracing from F.D.Roosevelt (3,185) right and up until the land stops it, M, sailing to the end; Join with Herbert Hoover and Detach again; Running the Gauntlet sailing north (sighting, encounter, fighting, battle lost messages); "In Port" (The Last Crown, 51,182); the wind's face (SCNF byte 19h set to 1 in the DOSBox copy); Race for the Crown with the squadron moved beside the enemy crown (48,8) in the DOSBox copy of S1: taken on tick 20, "You Won" blinking, a key goes straight to the battle report; The Bane of Oldain with all but two enemy squadrons removed: the offer to surrender, shown over the opponent's page. All matched the remake (`WAS_Remake/test/test_rules.c`) pixel for pixel apart from the enemy's moves. The DOSBox copies of S0, S1 and SCNF were put back afterwards.
- `screentext.py` needs `font8.txt` next to it: `python3 make_font8.py ../../../WAS_Original_Files_3.5/WAS.EXE > font8.txt`, copied to `WAR_Analysis/tools/dosbox` (not kept in either repo folder).
- Hercules (`CONF=dosbox_was_herc.conf`): the first campaign's start matches the remake's Hercules screen (`WAS_Remake/test/test_play.c`, p1_start_herc) pixel for pixel. DOSBox shows the lit pixels as grey AAAAAAh.
- Step 4 (the sea battle, 13 Nov): in the DOSBox copy of S0, GEO.WASHINGTON's squadron (16) moved to 44,145 and MACABRE's (7) to 44,141, and SCNF's fight delay set to 1000, so that the encounter comes at once and lasts: the marker to 44,145 (`wasgoto.py`), Z. Practice: F.D.Roosevelt (3,186), P. The battle's first screens (overview, close view) matched the remake (`WAS_Remake/test/test_battle.c`) pixel for pixel. Boarding: T twice (fast), MACABRE grapples GEO.WASHINGTON within 15 s, Z: the deck matched pixel for pixel. For timings, a conf with `cycledown=1200` drops from 1500 to 300 cycles with one Ctrl+F11 once the scene is reached; frames counted from screenshots every 0.1 s (a boarding frame 0.50 s while waiting, 0.6 s fighting). The DOSBox copies of S0 and SCNF were put back afterwards.
- Step 6 (the menus, 13 Nov): screenshots at 1500 cycles of the title page, the titles, the story, the rules, the opponents, the campaign menu, the opening, the Options Shoppe, the battle report (S, Y at once: Thor Foote astonished), the save pages (`setup_was_b.sh`) all matched the remake (`WAS_Remake/test/test_menu.c`) pixel for pixel, but for the Options Shoppe's data disk line (the remake names its folder). An SS saved there loads in the remake (`test_saved.c`: the map matched pixel for pixel), and the remake's SS loads in WAS.EXE. Timings from video at 300 cycles (`dosbox_was_xt.conf`, frames every 0.1 s): Enter on go to war, 1.2 s to the scroll, the unroll 0.3-0.4 s; leaving a scroll, scroll_clear 0.8 s, the roll-up 0.4 s; the title page waits 7.3 s for the demo; the quote stays 7.2 s (The Bane of Oldain) or 4.9 s (The Race for the Crown) before the map. At 300 cycles a held key repeats: tap keys (HOLD=0.15), or Enter chooses the next page too.
- The Tandy version (14 Nov, `setup_wast.sh`, DOSBox-X): `xrun.sh`, Return five times (about 2.5 s apart; the rules page and the opponents may need one more each), the first campaign's map in 16 colours. Practice with F.D.Roosevelt (`xgoto.py 3 186`, P): the battle is in the CGA 4-colour mode with palette registers 1 = light blue, 2 = brown. Compared with WAS.EXE's practice at the same moment in DOSBox 0.74 (which shows it as CGA palette 1) colour index by colour index: only the sea and land fills and 23 pixels of the gun differ. With `wast_move.py $DOS/wast/WAST.DAT 0 16 44 145 7 44 141` and SCNFT's fight delay (bytes 6-7) set to 1000: the encounter at once, `xgoto.py 44 145`, Z, T twice, MACABRE grapples GEO.WASHINGTON within 15 s, Z: the boarding in 16 colours. The DOSBox copies of WAST.DAT and SCNFT were put back afterwards.
- The Tandy explosion (14 Nov): a hit blows a ship up with chance 1 in 128, so the DOSBox copy of WAST.EXE was patched in its overlays (the overlay manager reads them from WAST.EXE, not WASTU.EXE): in shot_hits (ovl4:3034, file 2042Fh + 5) `cmp ax, 1` to `cmp ax, 200h` (every hit blows up), and in fire_guns (ovl4:23E0, file 1F7E0h) `mov al, 0FFh` to `mov al, 3` (every broadside blows up the enemy's ship 3, as `battle_test_blow_up` does in the remake). With the encounter above and SCNFT's fight delay 20000: Z, F, and the DOSBox-X picture grabbed in a loop with `import` (about 25 frames a second). The flashes show colours 1-3 as 10, 12, 14 with the BIOS's palette 0 and as 9, 6, 15 with palette 1. WAST.EXE, WAST.DAT and SCNFT were put back afterwards. (`xrun.sh` now also removes the X lock files `kill -9` leaves, without which a second Xvfb :99 did not start.)
