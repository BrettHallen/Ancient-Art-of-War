# The Ancient Art of War
Analysis of my favourite MS-DOS game by Brøderbund Software Inc.<br>

I played two of the games in the series in CGA so this is how I remember them:
- WAR: The Ancient Art of War (1984)
- WAS: The Ancient Art of War at Sea (1987)

I never played The Ancient Art of War in the Skies (1992).<br>

## [Ancient Art of War Remake](/WAR_Remake)
A modern version of the game in C and SDL3, using the original CGA or VGA graphics loaded from [your own copy of the game](/WAR_Original_Files_3.5). My main target is macOS but you could probably compile also for Linux & Windows.<br>

Work in progress.<br>
